# Changelog

## [1.9.1] - 2026-08-17

### Fixed

- Hovering an appearance or a set's pieces in the Appearances journal no longer stops your movement keys working.

## [1.9.0] - 2026-08-16

### Added

- **Filter your appearances by colour.** A strip of swatches above the grid on the Items tab narrows the page to pieces carrying that colour, both at the transmogrifier and in your collection.
  - Pieces showing the most of the colour come first.
  - A last swatch, Other Colours, keeps whatever no other swatch finds.
  - Roll a Random Piece puts a piece from the filtered page straight onto the slot.
- **Roll a random outfit at the transmogrifier.** A dice on the character preview spins every armour slot through the pieces you own and slows to a stop, and a padlock on each slot holds it where it is.
- **Keep a rolled outfit to one colour.** With a swatch lit on the Items tab, a second dice rolls every slot in that colour, and hides a slot you own nothing suitable for.
- **Recoloured sets share one row on the Extra Sets tab.** A season's armour sold under half a dozen names folds into a single row, with its number of colourways in the corner and your progress counted across all of them.

### Improved

- **Sets tab rows count colourways too.** A tier collected on Normal no longer reads as finished, since the count, the name colour and the bar follow every difficulty rather than the one furthest along.
- **New icon on the minimap button.** The button carries the addon's own artwork now, and the icons in the More from Lucky Phil row in settings are new to match.
- **New artwork on the roll buttons.** All three dice are drawn in the addon's own style, with the square button plate taken off so they sit flat like the padlocks.
