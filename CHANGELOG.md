# Changelog

## 1.5.0

Lucky's Wardrobe takes over from Lucky's Better Wardrobe. It is a ground-up rebuild that adds to Blizzard's collection journal instead of replacing it, so it is much lighter and far less likely to break when a patch lands. Your collected appearances are untouched, they live on Blizzard's side. Settings and saved data from Better Wardrobe do not carry over, and a few of its features have not been rebuilt yet.

### In the collection journal

- **Extra Sets tab**: a third tab beside Sets listing the armour sets Blizzard defines but never shows, narrowed to the ones your class can wear, ready to search, preview and track.
- **Sets you can buy**: the sets an ensemble teaches are listed too, close to two thousand of them, each one naming the ensemble to go after.
- **One garment, every colour**: an outfit the Trading Post sells in eighteen colours is one row with a colour picker, not eighteen rows of sweatsuit.
- **Sort and filter the Sets tab**: sort by completion, and narrow the list by expansion or by source, covering Raid, PvP, Covenants, Heritage, Cosmetic, Trading Post and Miscellaneous.
- **Sort and filter Extra Sets**: order the tab by name, completion, piece count or how many colours a set comes in, and narrow it by expansion or by what you have collected.
- **Track a whole set at once, or one piece of it**: shift-click a set to track every appearance you are still missing from it, or open the set and shift-click a single piece.
- **A crosshair on what you are hunting**: open a set and the pieces you are tracking carry a crosshair in the corner, on either sets tab.

### Out in the world

- **Sets you can finish here**: walk into a dungeon or raid to see which sets you are close to completing drop pieces there, which pieces you still need, and where each one comes from. It opens on its own, or on a keybinding or `/wardrobe sets`.
- **Loot alerts**: a sound or a chat message when you loot a piece of a set you are close to finishing.
- **Catalyst marks**: pieces you could catalyse from something already in your bags are marked in the set list. Requires Transmog Upgrade Master.

### At the transmogrifier

- **Only the sets you can wear**: the Sets tab no longer fills up with armour your character cannot wear, held there by a cloak that would fit. Turn it off from the tab's own Filter button to browse the lot again.
- **Extra Sets tab**: a tab beside Sets showing the hidden sets your class can wear, closest to complete first, and one click dresses you in every piece you own.
- **Set names on the cards**: every set card carries its name across the top, on the Sets, Custom Sets and Extra Sets tabs alike.
- **Random outfit**: hold the dice button to spin every armour slot through appearances you own, then let go and watch it settle.
- **Saved situations**: save the situation you have built up on one outfit and load it onto another.
- **Situations at a glance**: see an outfit's selected situation values on the list itself, or its full list in a tooltip when you hover it.
- **Keep your transmog tab**: switching outfits no longer throws you back to Items.

### Everywhere else

- **Set info on item tooltips**: hover an item anywhere and its tooltip names the set the piece belongs to and how much of that set you have, as in "From set: Glyphed Garb 7/8".
- **Wowhead addresses**: ctrl-click an item in your appearance collection for its Wowhead address, ready to copy.
- **Minimap button**: shift-click for the set list, right-click for settings.
- **A welcome note**: a hello at your first login, with the Discord address for anything the addon is still missing.
- **Conflict warning**: a dialog at login if Better Wardrobe or the old Lucky's Better Wardrobe is still enabled, with a button to turn either one off and reload on the spot.

### Known issues

- **The Extra Sets tab in the transmogrify window takes a second or two to settle.** Its cards dress as they load, so the tab looks half drawn at first. It sorts itself out, no reload needed.
