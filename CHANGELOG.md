# Changelog

## [1.8.1] - 2026-08-15

### Improved

- **New icon on the minimap button.** The button carries the addon's own artwork now, and the icons in the More from Lucky Phil row in settings are new to match.

## [1.8.0] - 2026-08-15

### Added

- **See what's new in the settings panel.** Settings opens on a What's New list, with a card for each setting added in recent releases that takes you to where it lives.

### Improved

- **Toggle Dev Mode and the minimap button from the title bar.** Both are icon buttons in the settings title bar now, in place of the General page that held them.
- **Read the addon's version in the settings title bar.** It sits beside the addon name, with the Lucky's Utils version in its tooltip, in place of the version rows at the bottom of the General page.
